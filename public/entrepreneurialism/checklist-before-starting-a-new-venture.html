Entrepreneurialism

A simple checklist before starting any new venture

By Waleed Khan · 13 November 2026

Most new ventures do not fail because the idea was terrible. They fail because something obvious was never checked. Before you spend money or time on a new business, answer these questions in writing. If you cannot answer one, find out before you start. For a business to succeed, there must be demand for the service or product. For a startup, it is very difficult to create new demand from scratch.

1. The problem

  • What problem does this solve, in one sentence?
  • Who has this problem today, and how are they handling it now?
  • Would they pay to have it solved, or is it only "nice to have"?

2. The customer

  • Who exactly is the first customer? Can you name five people or businesses?
  • Have you spoken to at least three of them?
  • Where will you find more of them?

3. The money

  • What does it cost to start, and what does it cost every month to keep running?
  • How much will you charge, and how many sales cover your costs?
  • How long can you last if sales are slow for six months?

4. Your time

  • How many hours a week will this take?
  • Which of your current businesses or commitments will get less of you?
  • Can it run for a week without you?

5. The people and the rules

  • Who do you need: a supplier, a partner, a first hire?
  • What licences, registrations or taxes apply where you operate?
  • Is there a written agreement with everyone you depend on?

6. The competition

  • Who else sells something similar, and what do they do well?
  • Why would a customer pick you over them?

7. The test

  • What is the smallest version you can sell next month?
  • What result would tell you to continue, and what result would tell you to stop?

8. The exit

  • If it does not work, how do you stop and how much do you lose?
  • Is that a loss you can accept?

How to use it

Write one or two lines under each question. If most answers are guesses, spend a few weeks turning them into facts. If you cannot answer a question after that, it is the one that will cause trouble later.
